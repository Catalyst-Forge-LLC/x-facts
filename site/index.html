Shared rules

Every label in the family keeps the same skeleton and the same teeth.

  • The Golden Rule Objective facts only. Subjective claims stay in READMEs and marketing pages.
  • undisclosed over omission Non-disclosure is itself a fact worth labeling. A column of undisclosed is a story.
  • Closed enums Judgment fields use fixed vocabularies so labels compare across products.
  • CC0 specs, MIT tooling Specs and schemas are public domain. Generators and validators are MIT. Nothing to hesitate over.

How a label earns a domain

The suite grows only when all three hold. Saying no in public is part of the brand.

  1. Someone adopts or installs the thing and needs to trust it at that moment.
  2. The essential facts are objective and at least partly machine-derivable.
  3. No incumbent format already answers the question well.
